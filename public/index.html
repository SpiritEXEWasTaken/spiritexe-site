Lanternfall

Meta Quest 3Co-op, up to 5Beta

Mine an endless cave under a giant glass lantern with your friends. Haul ore back up, upgrade your gear, and keep the Lantern lit when the monsters come out of the trees.

  • Dig deeper for rarer ore, with pickaxes and drills
  • Fly your backpack thrusters, or walk and teleport
  • Fight off waves of monsters with the Lantern's light beam
  • Public lobbies, or private ones with a code

Download the beta

Lanternfall is in a closed beta. Enter your beta key to download the newest build.

The download is an Android APK. Install it using whatever method you use (for example SideQuest).

CleanMyVR

Meta QuestWindows, macOS, LinuxOpen source

Debloat, clean up, back up and fix a Meta Quest over USB, with a guided app on your computer. No root and nothing to sideload, every change can be undone, and your games keep working.

  • Remove Meta's extra apps and telemetry, with a preview first
  • Back up photos, videos and game saves, checked file by file
  • Free up storage and fix the "Home is just a white sky" bug
  • Recheck spots what Meta quietly reinstalled and puts it right
