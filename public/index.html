Lanternfall

  • Meta Quest 3
  • Co-op, up to 5 players
  • Closed beta

Mine an endless cave under a giant glass lantern with your friends. Haul ore back up, upgrade your gear, and keep the Lantern lit when the monsters come out of the trees.

  • Dig deeper for rarer ore, with pickaxes and drills
  • Fly your backpack thrusters, or walk and teleport
  • Fight off waves of monsters with the Lantern’s light beam
  • Public lobbies, or private ones with a code

Download the Beta

Lanternfall is in a closed beta. Enter your beta key to download the newest build.

You’ll get an Android APK. Install it on your Quest with SideQuest, or however you usually sideload.

PipeVR

Video app, version 1.0

One app for YouTube, Twitch and a dozen other video sites, made for VR headsets. No ads, SponsorBlock built in, and a real VR mode.

  • YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Patreon, Floatplane, Rumble, Nebula and more, with one search across all of them
  • Sign in to YouTube with a TV code for your subscriptions, history, playlists and comments
  • 360° and 180° video, 3D, and theater screens in eight surroundings, from a movie theater to your own room
  • Live chat as a panel you can move and resize, plus captions
Download APK for PipeVR Install with FrameDrop Source on GitHub for PipeVR

For Meta Quest, Pico, Steam Frame and other Android headsets. Install the APK with SideQuest or adb. On a Steam Frame, use FrameDrop. Free and open source (GPLv3), built on PipePipe.

CleanMyVR

Quest cleanup tool

Debloat, clean up, back up and fix a Meta Quest over USB, with a guided app on your computer. No root and nothing to sideload, every change can be undone, and your games keep working.

  • Remove Meta’s extra apps and telemetry, with a preview first
  • Back up photos, videos and game saves, checked file by file
  • Free up storage and fix the “Home is just a white sky” bug
  • Recheck spots what Meta quietly reinstalled and puts it right
Download CleanMyVR Source on GitHub for CleanMyVR

For Windows, macOS and Linux. Tested on Meta Quest 3: turn on Developer Mode, plug the headset in, and start the app. Free and open source (MIT).
